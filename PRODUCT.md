# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners of any size and in any location who need a website, internal tools, analytics or SEO help and are not necessarily technical. The studio is based in Edmonton, Alberta, and the site currently names four audiences: trades and construction, professional services, retail and hospitality, and growing small businesses. The visitor's job is to judge whether this is someone they can trust and then get in touch.

## Product Purpose

yanmiAde builds websites and software, turns business data into decisions, and helps companies get found online. The site exists to bring in enquiries through the contact form or hello@yanmife.ca. Success is a qualified message from a visitor who understands what is offered and who they will be dealing with.

## Positioning

One person, start to finish. Visitors talk only to Yanmife (who also goes by Grace), never a sales team. Plans and prices are clear before any work starts, the work is hand-built with nothing extra, and fixes and changes continue after launch. Honest advice includes saying when a client does not need something fancy.

## Operating Context

The first step is a message through the contact form or email. A reply follows within two business days with questions and honest first thoughts, then a short chat, then a plain plan with a clear price. Services cover website design and development, SEO, business analytics, automation and internal tools, AI workflow automation, system integrations, customer and staff portals, data migration, website rescue and consulting.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Hand-built static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS with no build step. Pages: home, services, about, contact, privacy, terms, 404.
- Hosted on the custom domain yanmife.ca (see CNAME). Contact form goes through Web3Forms; cookie-free analytics via Cloudflare Web Analytics. Both still need their keys or tokens added before launch.
- Self-hosted fonts in `assets/fonts/`.
- Privacy and terms pages still contain placeholders to fill in.

## Brand Commitments

- The name is spelled **yanmiAde** (lowercase y, capital A) and pronounced YAHN-mee-AH-deh. The founder goes by Yanmife, with Grace as an easy alternative. Do not respell either name.
- Tagline in use: "Innovation starts with yanmiAde."
- Copy voice is plain and honest: plain words, direct advice, no filler.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real work screenshots from the Degner project: `assets/degner-site.webp`, `assets/degner-mobile.webp`, `assets/degner-logo.png`.
- Founder background as stated on the About page: finance and computer science, some time in construction.
- No testimonials, client lists, case studies, pricing or benchmarks exist. Future work must not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Show real work and real facts only; absence of proof is better than fabricated proof.
2. Make the person behind the studio visible; the single-point-of-contact promise is the product.
3. Say it plainly. If a sentence would fit any agency, rewrite it.
4. Keep the path to one clear contact action short on every page.
5. Stay simple and fast: hand-built, no unnecessary dependencies.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience includes non-technical visitors, so language must be plain. Meet WCAG AA contrast, respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, and keep the site usable at all common mobile widths.
