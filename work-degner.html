Degner Construction

A new custom website for an Alberta infrastructure contractor. In final review, with a coming-soon page live meanwhile.

15+pages, plus a separate coming-soon site
4forms, with resume upload on the careers forms
~90images, resized for fast loading
0paid themes, page builders or software subscriptions
01

The brief

Present Degner clearly to customers, applicants and partners, replacing an older WordPress site.

02

What we built

Service pages, a team page, safety, careers with resume upload, quote requests and site search.

03

How it works

Hand-built plain pages, no theme or page builder, installed on Degner's hosting through a small plugin we wrote. Little to break or pay for.

Highlights

Details that make a site feel finished

  • Original design in black and white to match the brand, not a template
  • Custom team page with a photo grid, carousel and department groups
  • Careers and contact forms that check the email address, and a resume upload on the careers forms
  • Search written for the site that finds pages as you type
  • Cookie-free visitor statistics, so no cookie banner is needed
